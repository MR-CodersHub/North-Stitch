Before you ask

Common questions

What is your minimum order?
50 pieces per style and colourway for embroidery. Smaller sample runs are available once a program is established — the stitched pre-production sample itself is always included.
How fast can you deliver?
Standard production is 7–10 working days from artwork approval, plus transit. Rush schedules are possible on core garments — tell us the date in the quote builder and we'll confirm feasibility up front.
Do you charge for logo digitizing?
No. Digitizing, thread matching and the pre-production sample are part of the program. You approve a physical stitched sample before any bulk run starts.
Can you match colours across garment styles?
Yes. We build a thread and garment colour standard for your brand and keep it on file, so a polo, a jacket and a hi-vis vest all carry the same mark.
Do you handle multi-location reorders?
That's the point of the account portal. Approved garments, saved artwork and per-location ordering mean each site reorders against the same specification — no re-quoting, no drift.
